Frequently Asked Questions

Is Price It an AI tool?

It's AI-assisted and expert-reviewed. Our proprietary technology prepares your breakdown, and our gemologists verify every figure before it reaches you.

Is the estimate accurate?

It's a real-world estimate, not a guess. The calculator on this page uses published rates and the standard gold weight for each category. Your full report is built from your actual design, live gold and diamond rates and a gemologist's review, then itemised into metal, stones, making and GST. Final numbers can shift slightly with final specifications.

What jewellery can I upload?

Almost anything you'd wear or gift: rings, necklaces, pendants, earrings, bracelets, bangles and any diamond-set piece. A photo, a screenshot, a Pinterest save or a sketch all work.

Can I compare natural and lab-grown diamonds?

Yes, and this is where most people are surprised. The calculator above prices both side by side across every metal and clarity, and your report does the same for your exact design.

Can I customise my estimate?

Yes. Change the metal, colour, carat weight, diamond type or clarity and it re-prices instantly. The estimate is a starting point you shape until it's true to you.

Is my image secure?

Yes. Your images are used only to prepare your estimate, never shared, never sold. Your design stays yours.

How long does the full report take?

Within 24 hours. Free, itemised and expert-checked.
