3Soul · Homepage

A healthier page with four holes in it.

The homepage is in markedly better shape than the estimator. Nothing fails contrast, nothing runs away with the network, and the one form on it is labelled correctly. What it has instead is a short list of specific breakages, a press section that renders nothing, a heading that says nothing, controls too small to hit, and half the page spent making one offer twice.

Recommendation

Three repairs, then two decisions

The homepage does not need rebuilding, which is why this audit stops at Flagged. It needs a short list of fixes and two questions answered about what the page is for.

Repairs, an afternoon

Findings 1, 2 and 9. Put the press logos back on screen, give the page a readable H1, and correct the Bengaluru URL. None is a design question, and together they change what the page says about itself: certified, credible, and findable.

A pass over the controls, a day

Finding 3, with 7 alongside it. 161 controls under 44×44 and 16 text elements under 12 px, most of both in the footer and the carousel. This is padding and type scale, not layout, but it touches every screen, so it wants doing in one deliberate pass.

Two decisions, worth a conversation

Findings 4 and 5. Does the homepage make the estimate offer once or twice, and does the hero lead with 4.4 MB of video? Both are defensible as they stand. Neither should stay by default, and merging the two pitches would return about two screens.

Then the rest

Findings 6, 8 and 10. Name and defer the Instagram frames, shorten the footer, and take an inventory of the 84 scripts. Housekeeping, in the sense that none of it is urgent and all of it is worth knowing.

On priority between the two pages. If only one gets attention, it is the estimator, its findings are larger and its traffic is more intent-heavy. The homepage’s value here is that three of its ten findings are cheap enough to do while the larger work is still being decided.

Checked, and not flagged

Three things were measured, came back clean, and are recorded here so the absence is deliberate rather than an oversight.

  • Contrast. 262 text elements checked against their resolved backgrounds; none below its threshold. The self-test on the ratio function returns 21.00 for black on white and 1.00 for white on white, so the zero is a result rather than a broken measurement.
  • The marquee. The four phrases scroll past three times, but two copies carry aria-hidden="true", a screen reader meets the line once. Correct as built.
  • The newsletter field. The one input on the page has a real <label for> pointing at it. This is the pattern the estimator’s six fields are missing.
