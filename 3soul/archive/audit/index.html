3Soul · Price It

Bringing the estimate onto the page.

Price It draws the right traffic, and the service behind it is strong. The page doesn’t yet put a figure on screen — the title, the search intent and the closing button all point to one, and the report follows a day later. This is a review of the page as it stands, and a recommendation for where to take it.

What the page is carrying

Asset weight, taken from each file’s Content-Length rather than from one page load — so neither cache nor scroll position can move it. Video is not a contributing factor here; it is the page.

Video · 425 MB
10 video files, 425 MB — largest 79.5 MB 39 images, 6.6 MB 36 scripts, 5.1 MB — about 842 KB over the wire

Browsers stream video rather than fetch it whole, so nobody pays 425 MB at once — they pay continuously, for as long as the page is open. Left alone without a single click, transfer climbed from 3.2 MB to 9.9 MB and requests from 265 to 381. 121 of those fire after load; 18 go to a session-recording script.

Speed: the lab and the field disagree

Our own paint timings were unusable — 2.13 s, 9.04 s and 39.02 s across three loads of the same URL, which is the measuring environment rather than the page. So here is Lighthouse on a defined connection instead, run 29 Aug 2026, mobile, emulated Moto G Power on Slow 4G. This is the homepage, 3soul.in — the estimator carries more video, so it will not be better.

Both are true. Returning visitors on decent phones, with the video and fonts already cached, get a fast page — which is why Core Web Vitals pass and why there is no ranking penalty today. A first-time visitor on a mid-range Android over slow 4G waits 11.2 seconds for the main content. That is the visitor the estimator is meant to convert.

It also settles the earlier question: the spread in our own numbers was cache, not noise. The field figure is the one to defend; the lab figure is the one to fix.

    Supporting

    Recommendation

    We’d suggest Approach A, in two phases — with the Rebuilt fixes shipping regardless.

    Give the price on the page, then ask.

    It is the only option where the page does what its own title says. Search intent, the headline and the button finally agree, and the contact request stops being a toll gate and becomes an upgrade.

    Phase one
    • Calculator runs in the page.
    • Rates in a table staff update weekly.
    • No new infrastructure. Ships fast.
    • Accepted: rates drift between updates, and the formula is readable by anyone who looks.
    Phase two
    • Pricing moves behind a small service — formula stays private.
    • Gold rate updates itself.
    • Leads land somewhere real, with a WhatsApp alert.
    • Optional: phase one stands on its own if this never happens.
    Either way
    • All fifteen from Rebuilt.
    • None of them commit to a direction — they are improvements either way.
    • Best not held up by the A/B/C decision.

    Measurement

    Worth instrumenting before anything changes, so the next version of this conversation has real numbers in it rather than opinions.

    Events to add

    • estimator_view — reached the tool at all.
    • config_change — touched any control.
    • estimate_shown — saw a number, and how long it took.
    • gate_view — reached the contact ask.
    • lead_submit — with which fields filled.
    • whatsapp_click — the path that skips the form.

    What we’d watch

    • Drop-off between estimate_shown and gate_view — the real test of Approach A.
    • Share of leads arriving by WhatsApp rather than the form.
    • Time from landing to first estimate.
    • Mobile against desktop, separately. They are different products today.
